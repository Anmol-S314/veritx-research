Subject: FIX FOUND: Preemptive VC (Benini 2026, arXiv 2607.01430) — attacks Coffman no-preemption at the shared bridge

The bidirectional bridge starvation (A->B 11% vs B->A 82%) is a traffic-class
interaction deadlock at the SHARED bridge link — not a routing bug. 2026 ETH
paper (Leone/Colagrande/Benini, FlooNoC v0.8.0) formalizes exactly this:
"protocol-level dependencies create circular waits even when routing is
deadlock-free." Their fix: PREEMPTIVE VC — round-robin link ownership; a
stream whose downstream isn't ready gets PREEMPTED next cycle. Attacks
Coffman's no-preemption condition (no prior VC design did). Cheapest:
+3% area, no freq loss.

VC isolation (my c9ab55e) failed because it attacks mutual exclusion and
broke credit correlation. CreditBased (our current scheme) needs FIFO>=3 —
we have 8, so buffer depth isn't the issue; the missing piece is preemption.

round-robin over the two directions). Doc: docs/research/preemptive-vc-bridge-fix.md.
