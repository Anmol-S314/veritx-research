# Topology RTL Completion Matrix

**Status checked:** 2026-11-08
**Purpose:** Track family-faithful RTL, not merely whether a family has a graph model or generic RTL preview.

## Acceptance rule

A family is complete only when emitted or maintained RTL preserves its topology's physical connectivity, endpoint attachment/injection/ejection semantics, routing law, channel and VC behavior, and flow-control contract, and passes an end-to-end RTL simulation with packet/flit conservation, destination correctness, and drain. Lint, a route-table proof, a model/backend run, or a generic point-to-point lowering is useful evidence but is not family RTL acceptance. Performance comparisons additionally require identical traffic and an explicit resource/cost accounting.

## Current evidence

- The product preview emitter (`scripts/rtlgen/gen_rtl.py`, `dse/veritx_dse/product/rtl_export.py`) emits 64-bit, two-VC free/escape routers over connected reciprocal point-to-point graphs. Its own manifest states that family routing law, compiler controls, endpoint adaptation, and shared-wire semantics are not generally realized.
- Research graph RTL prototypes exist for mesh4, torus4, ring8, flatfly k=4/n=2, and GEC Express k=4. They normalize routing and port numbering to that emitter. An identical 64-packet trace passed on mesh4, torus4, flatfly and GEC Express: each delivered 256/256 flits with no wrong destinations or outstanding traffic. This demonstrates the graph bridge, not those families' native RTL.
- `rtl/t3/` contains retained mesh-derived 2D/3D RTL, but its README records multicast corner failures for the 2D design, calls 3D functional-only, and marks 4D incomplete/stub. It is not blanket acceptance evidence for the catalog.
- `rtl/ring_native/` now contains a 64-router ready/valid fabric with two one-entry VC buffers per input and native `+1/-1` wrap routing. Verilator checked 8,192 route cases and passed 4,096 one-flit source/destination packets with exact payload, all endpoints, deterministic backpressure, and full drain (2,949 cycles). This is a validated topology-faithful **single-flit prototype**, not the product's full multi-flit/BookSim router contract or performance evidence.
- `rtl/torus_native/` contains a point-to-point torus with wrap links and dateline VC transition. The K=5, 25-router version passed all 625 ordered one-flit pairs with deterministic egress stalls and drain (116 cycles). The canonical default k=8,n=2 (64 routers) now independently passes all 4,096 ordered one-flit pairs with backpressure and drain (577 cycles). The repair corrected the testbench's initial VC seed to match the actual first minimal dimension-order wrap crossing; the RTL route-transition logic was unchanged. This remains a single-flit prototype, not the full packet/VC contract or performance evidence.
- `rtl/tree4_native/` contains the 28-router/64-terminal multi-root Tree4 graph and union-valued upward route choices. Verilator passed 1,792 route-law checks and all 4,096 one-flit pairs with exact delivery, 491 observed backpressure cycles, and drain (8,227 cycles). This is not the separate structured `tree4` identity or its full packet/VC contract.
- `rtl/fly_native/` contains the 48-router, 128-directed-link BookSim fly graph with distinct injection/ejection attachments. Verilator passed all 4,096 one-flit endpoint pairs, backpressure, and drain (1,774 cycles). This does not establish multi-flit/VC equivalence.
- `rtl/qtree_native/` contains the 21-router QTree graph with all 64 terminals attached four per leaf. Verilator passed all 4,096 ordered pairs across destination waves with concurrent sources, egress backpressure, conservation, and drain. This is not the separate structured `qtree` identity or its full packet/VC contract.
- `rtl/structured_qtree_native/` contains the distinct 21-router/21-endpoint breadth-first 4-ary tree with one terminal per router. Independent Verilator acceptance passed all 441 ordered pairs, with exact delivery, 494 observed egress stalls, and complete drain (743 cycles). Routing walks the destination's parent chain to select the correct child path. The one-flit prototype does not claim multi-flit/VC equivalence.
- `rtl/gec_mecs_native/` contains a standalone shared-channel primitive plus a connected `gec_mecs16` single-flit unicast fabric: 16 routers/endpoints, 16 shared row and 16 shared column wires with three taps each. The fabric follows BookSim ascending peer-tap order and X-then-Y `dor_gec`; its 256 ordered pairs, same-destination concurrent case, payload conservation, backpressure and drain pass. The primitive also passes directed selector/credit checks. These prototypes do not establish configured multi-flit/3-VC allocation and credit behavior, multicast header semantics, or physical qualification.
- `rtl/gec_mesh_native/` implements the configured `gec_mesh_k8` nearest-neighbor graph (64 routers/endpoints, 112 undirected links) as a point-to-point mesh with row-major IDs and X-then-Y routing. Independent Verilator acceptance passed 4,096/4,096 ordered one-flit pairs and full drain (882 cycles). No wraparound or express links are present; the prototype omits multi-flit framing and VCs.
- `rtl/mesh_native/` contains a distinct 4x4, 16-router/16-terminal XY mesh prototype. Independent Verilator acceptance delivered all 256 ordered one-flit pairs exactly once and drained (98 cycles). It is a single-flit prototype without the configured packet/VC contract.
- `rtl/concentrated_mesh_native/` contains the canonical 4x4 mesh with four terminal seats per router (16 routers, 64 terminals) and deterministic XY routing. Fresh Verilator acceptance passed all 4,096 one-flit pairs, with 10 contention cycles, 15 blocked cycles, 10 stalls, and complete drain (1,331 cycles). Multi-flit framing and the configured VC contract are not implemented.
- `rtl/fat_tree_native/` targets the explicitly small `fat_tree4` preset (four routers and four terminals, radix 2, two tiers), not `fattree16`. Fresh Verilator acceptance passed all 16 route checks and all 16 one-flit source/destination pairs exactly once, with 17 stalls and complete drain. Multi-flit/VC behavior is not implemented.
- `rtl/fattree16_native/` targets the supported 8-router/16-terminal AnyNet bipartite graph (four edge routers, four upper routers, 16 edge-core links, four terminals per edge router). The prototype uses deterministic min-hop routing on that graph. Fresh Verilator acceptance passed all 256 ordered one-flit pairs exactly once, with 131 observed stalls, ready/valid payload stability while stalled, and complete drain (166 cycles). It is not equivalent to the separate native `fattree16.cfg` NCA routing or the project's multi-flit/VC contract.
- `rtl/fattree_native_nca/` separately targets the canonical BookSim `fattree` k=4,n=2 network (8 routers, 16 terminals, 4 terminals per bottom router, complete bipartite inter-tier links) and `fattree_nca` route behavior. Fresh independent Verilator acceptance passed all 256 ordered one-flit pairs exactly once, with 116 observed stalls, ready/valid payload stability, and drain (199 cycles). The prototype deterministically selects the destination's bottom-router parent among the four legal uplinks; BookSim's route function randomly selects among those links. This does not implement multi-flit/VC behavior or claim timing equivalence.
- `rtl/flatfly_native/` contains the canonical 4-ary, 2-dimensional flattened butterfly at concentration 1 (16 routers/endpoints, 96 directed channels), matching the repository's k=4,n=2 `flattened_butterfly16` preset graph and least-significant-coordinate-first DOR route law. Fresh verification checked reciprocal port adjacency and all 256 routes, then delivered all 256 one-flit pairs exactly once under deterministic egress stalls, asserted valid/payload stability across egress stalls, and drained (65 cycles). This is distinct from the staged BookSim `fly` network and is not multi-flit/VC RTL.
- `rtl/gec_express_native/` targets supported `gec_express16` (`k=4,c=1`, 16 routers/endpoints, 96 directed express channels). Fresh Verilator acceptance delivered all 256 one-flit pairs exactly once with backpressure and drained (50 cycles). This is family-faithful topology/route behavior for the pinned instance, not full configured BookSim router/VC timing equivalence.

## Work order

1. Establish shared router and endpoint contracts, then complete the simpler point-to-point families with their native routing: mesh, torus, ring, flatfly, GEC Express.
2. Add endpoint-structure families: concentrated mesh, fat-tree, QTree, Tree4, Fly.
3. Integrate shared resources: GEC MECS and hybrid, then connect and qualify the complete SROTA fabric.
4. Keep unsupported or under-specified cases as explicit typed refusals. Do not fill contract gaps by guessing.

## Known specification blocker

TOPO-003's MECS channel credit mask selects bitmap targets when the multicast bitmap is nonzero. Its drop match expression is destination-index equality OR bitmap membership. The reviewed ROUTE-001/VC-002/TOPO-003 text does not explicitly require the destination index to be included in the multicast bitmap. The local SROTA README records this hazard. Preserve the literal extracted RTL until the architecture contract resolves the mismatch; add tests for both the specified expression and the chosen resolution once agreed.

## Completion status

**The user's all-topology RTL goal is not complete.** Verified single-flit topology prototypes now include mesh (16 endpoints), concentrated mesh (64 terminals), GEC mesh k=8 (64 endpoints), Ring, Torus K=5/K=8, `fat_tree4`, the supported `fattree16_anynet` graph prototype (min-hop), native BookSim fat-tree k=4,n=2 NCA graph/routing, flattened_butterfly k=4,n=2,c=1 (same structured preset graph and DOR route), GEC Express, connected GEC MECS16 shared-wire unicast, Tree4-native, Fly-native, QTree-native, structured Tree4, structured QTree, structured `dragonfly4`, and native BookSim `dragonflynew` k=2,n=1. The GEC MECS16 prototype preserves 16 row and 16 column shared wires with three taps each and passes all 256 ordered pairs, same-destination concurrency, backpressure, conservation and drain; it does not establish configured multi-flit/3-VC allocation/credit behavior or multicast packet-header semantics. The dragonflynew behavioral fabric matches all 180 BookSim-directed channels and the `dragonfly_port` next hops over 5,184 endpoint pairs, then passes payload delivery, egress backpressure stability, and drain; it is centralized and single-flit, not explicit physical-link/router RTL or the configured 5-flit/4-VC implementation. The native NCA prototype adds 256/256 all-pairs delivery, 116 egress stalls with ready/valid stability, and drain, but deterministically chooses among the legal parent links instead of matching BookSim's random choice. The flatfly/flattened-butterfly prototype adds 256/256, egress stall stability, and drain at 65 cycles. These prototypes do not yet implement each configured multi-flit, VC, allocator, and flow-control contract. SROTA has no connected fabric because key router/channel binding semantics remain unresolved, and GEC hybrid has no RTL because its runtime downstream-credit observability/timing contract is not yet mapped and validated; its typed blocker and unblocking checks are documented in `rtl/gec_hybrid_native/README.md`. Fixture/aggregate entries remain explicitly non-target identities. The `dragonfly4` structured preset and native BookSim's 72-terminal `dragonflynew` are separate implementations and are not conflated.
