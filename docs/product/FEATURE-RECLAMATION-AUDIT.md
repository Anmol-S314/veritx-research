### **The explicit answer §3 demands**

> What scientifically meaningful functionality would be lost if Phase-3
> Static Evaluate were implemented without reclaiming Wave E?

**Wave-E itself would not be lost — it already runs.** What would be lost is
its **product projection**. The product surfaces `completion_cycles`,
`completion_ns` and `completion_time`. Wave-E computes **seven** metrics. A
Static Evaluate built on the current metric registry alone would surface one
aggregate window number and silently drop:

- `makespan` and `critical_path` — the schedule-shape facts
- `resource_utilization` — where the fabric is idle versus saturated
- `request_latency` and its distribution — the per-request view
- `ttft` and `decode_step_latency` — the serving-relevant latencies
- `sensitivity` — the counterfactual "what would move this number" analysis

and it would lose the **honesty boundary** with them: `fidelity_warning`, the
`unsupported` list, and `predictive_validation: NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Those are
what stop an analytical estimate being read as a measured claim. Dropping
them is not a UI regression; it is a **scientific claim regression**.

**Classification:** Wave-E `CANONICAL_NOW` (P3). Wave-E **metric exposure**
`NEEDS_CANONICALIZATION` (**P0**).

**Verdict on the thesis:** the *execution* half of the thesis —

The thesis is therefore **partially supported**: SROTA can compile, verify,
execute, compare, visualise and parameter-optimise a named-family fabric, and
can route/project/visualise a custom one — but it **cannot generate** a
custom fabric, **cannot declare** one as intent, and **cannot optimise over**
topology structure.

## Verdict

Coverage accounting now closes: **43 required domains, 30 audited, 13
partial, 0 unaudited.**

But `unaudited_domain_count == 0` is necessary and not sufficient. Four
deliverables the brief requires were **not produced** — the Wave-F parity
matrix (C), the Studio parity table (F), the example/regression parity table
(G), and the serving control list — and the 13 partial domains were located
but not traced to the required depth. Several global claims remain
unprovable while that is true.

Three things this pass establishes with confidence:

1. **The current tree is richer than pass 1 credited.** Wave-E, physical
   estimation, signed manifests, UVM generation, Verilator execution, a
   validation harness, ASTRA, Ramulator and a 13-module serving stack are
   present and mostly sealed. The "narrowed away" story applies to
   *synthesis and topology declaration*, not to the backend.
2. **Three registry rows contradict sealed canonical code** (`ROUTE-008`,
   `ROUTE-009`, `MEM-007`), while `MEM-006` and `ROUTE-011` are accurate.
   Same label, opposite verdicts.
3. **The explicit Wave-E answer:** Phase 3 without reclaiming Wave-E's
   product projection loses six of seven metrics *and* the honesty boundary
   that keeps analytical estimates from reading as measurements.
